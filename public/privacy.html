Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

SynergyHubs is currently in a testing phase. Features may change, and this policy will be updated as they do.

SynergyHubs (synergyhubs.org) helps students and teachers organize class group projects. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and whether you use SynergyHubs as a student or a teacher.
  • Password: if you sign up with an email and password, we store your password only as a one-way encrypted hash, never as readable text.
  • Google Sign-In: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, Google account ID, and profile picture link from Google. We never see your Google password, and if you create your account with Google, we do not create or store any password for it.
  • Content you add: classes, projects, milestones, submissions, status updates, work logs, and the links to resources and workspaces that you share.
  • Google Drive files you choose: when you attach a file using the Google Drive picker, we save only that file's name and link. We cannot see, read, or change anything else in your Google Drive.
  • Project activity: whether, how often, and when you open a project, so teachers can see team engagement.

How we store your information

Your username, password hash, and all other account data are stored in our own database on servers we manage. Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay logged in; signing out removes it. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in. New accounts are reviewed and approved by a SynergyHubs administrator before they can be used.
  • To show your classes, projects, and team activity to you and the people you work with.
  • To let teachers follow their students' project progress.

We do not sell your personal information, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Your project teammates can see your name, email, roles, and status updates on projects you share.
  • Your class's teacher or owner can see your project activity, status updates, and work logs for projects in their class.
  • Work logs are private to you and your teacher; other students cannot see them.
  • SynergyHubs administrators can see your name, email address, account type, and how you sign in, so they can approve or remove accounts.

We do not share personal information with anyone else, except where required by law.

Google user data

SynergyHubs uses Google Sign-In to authenticate you and the Google Drive picker (with access limited to the files you choose) to attach files. SynergyHubs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

SynergyHubs is not directed to children under 13. We do not ask for your age or date of birth when you sign up. If you are under 13, please do not create an account without a parent's or school's consent.

If an account is reported to us as belonging to a child under 13 without that consent, we will delete the account and its personal information. Parents, guardians, and schools can report an account by emailing support@synergyhubs.org.

Keeping or deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is active. To have your account and its data deleted, email support@synergyhubs.org from the address on your account and we will delete it. Because the service is in testing, data may occasionally be reset.

Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and access controls that limit project data to its members. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and post the new version on this page.

Contact us

Questions or requests: support@synergyhubs.org
